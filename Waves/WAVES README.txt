Game Name   : WAVES  
Team Name   : TARUINE  
Course Code : CSD1451s25  
High Concept: Navigate a dark maze using wave-generating rocks to reveal your path and lure enemies away.

-------------------------------------------
            How To Play
-------------------------------------------
In WAVES, you are trapped in a dark maze with restricted vision. Your only tools are rocks that generate waves of light and sound. Throw rocks to illuminate hidden areas and lure patrolling enemies away. Avoid being caught while locating keycards to unlock the exit.

Use the environment, vision mechanics, and enemy behavior to your advantage. Survive and escape!

The game includes:
- 3 progressively difficult levels: Easy, Medium, Hard.

-------------------------------------------
               Controls
-------------------------------------------
W / A / S / D      - Move the player  
Right Click        - Throw rock  
Spacebar           - Generate directional wave (based on mouse position)  
E                  - Interact (pick up rock/keycard, enter win zone)  
Esc                - Pause menu / Quit to main menu

Note: Directional waves can only be triggered in four directions relative to the player — Up, Down, Left, and Right.
